## New
- Fresh modern look: Archivo font, 13 colour themes, background styles (Soft glow, Silk lines, Dots) and real dark mode
- New navigation bar in your theme colour. Hold the + button to start Focus
- Home: greeting with sun or moon, search everything, calendar, quick actions, Stats and Focus side by side, next 7 days
- Calendar page with deadlines for every day
- Projects: swipe right to complete, Completed dropdown, hold for quick options, cover photos and file attachments (up to 30 MB, 2 GB storage)
- Project page: tap any box to change status, category, dates, priority or reminder
- To-dos: Urgent, High, Medium, Low and None priorities, your own categories, sub-tasks dropdown, select several to change or delete
- Notes: folders, tabs inside a note, bullet points, text size and bold, link previews, images and PDFs
- Preferences now use dropdowns and include tags, statuses and categories
- Send feedback straight to the developer, and a How to use guide
- Redesigned Backup & sync page, restore from a file, and storage use
- All app notifications also show in your phone's notification panel
